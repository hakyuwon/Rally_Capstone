# 🏸 Rally — Capstone Design

## Service Introduction

<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="1" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/550f99ca-26bd-4b0f-ae75-1217f17791aa" />

Finding badminton partners is harder than it looks —
clubs feel too formal, and pickup games have no structure.

**Rally** is a social badminton platform for Gen Z
that matches you with the right partner, tracks your game with a wearable,
and helps you grow with every rally.
No referees needed. No clubs required. Just play.

<br/>

## Service Feature

<img width="2880" height="1620" alt="7" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d642e2a8-52ea-4417-b88d-d5c6c5c6e495" />

Based on the full flow of experiencing a badminton game,
we identified key pain points at every stage —
finding the right partner, tracking scores without a referee, and feeling rewarded after a match.

**Rally** was built to solve exactly these problems,
making every step of the game more connected, measurable, and motivating.

<br/>
